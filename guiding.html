The Person Behind the Stories

History Is
Never Just
the Past.

I'm Atilla Yurttaş, a licensed professional tour guide based in Selçuk, near the ancient city of Ephesus. For more than 17 years, I've had the privilege of introducing travelers to the extraordinary cultural and historical heritage of Türkiye.

From the ancient civilizations of Anatolia to the living traditions of its towns and cities, my aim is to make history feel human, relevant and alive.

I believe that a meaningful journey is not simply about visiting famous landmarks. It is about understanding the people, ideas and events that shaped them.
