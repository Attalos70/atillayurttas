About
Me.

A life shaped by light, people and places.

I'm Atilla Yurttaş, a photographer based in Selçuk, Türkiye.

My journey with photography began in 2007. What started as a fascination with capturing moments gradually became a way of understanding the world around me.

Over the years, I have photographed ancient cities, quiet villages, crowded streets and the rhythms of everyday life. Along the way, I discovered that the most meaningful stories are often found in the smallest, most ordinary moments.

I'm drawn to natural light, human expressions, fleeting gestures and the atmosphere of places. Whether working in color or black and white, I look for images that hold something beyond what is immediately visible.

Photography, to me, is both a personal journey and an ongoing conversation with the world.

My Perspective
